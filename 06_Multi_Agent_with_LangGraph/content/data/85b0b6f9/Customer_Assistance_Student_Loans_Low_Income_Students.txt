2. **Federal vs. Private Loans**: If you are a low-income student, it is advisable to focus on federal student loans first. 3. **Grants and Scholarships**: I encourage you to also explore grants and scholarships that are specifically aimed at low-income students. Unlike loans, these funds do not require repayment and can significantly lower the total amount you may need to borrow.

4. **Loan Repayment Plans**: After graduation, various repayment options are available to help make student loan repayments more manageable based on your financial situation. Options like graduated repayment and income-driven repayment plans can offer flexibility tailored to your needs.

5. **Financial Counseling**: Many colleges and universities provide financial counseling services that can support low-income students in understanding their loan options and creating an effective budget for their education journey.
